IT Audit-Compliance Guidance Dashboard — Accounting

Continuously monitors the FTC and IRS (regulatory) plus AICPA, NIST, CISA, and Michigan state sources for new or revised guidance affecting CPA firms, tax preparers, and bookkeepers. Each item gets both a suggested IT audit-program change and a proactive client-readiness advisory, to help accounting clients meet the FTC Safeguards Rule and IRS written information security plan (WISP) expectations ahead of filing season.

This repository is maintained automatically by a weekly scheduled check plus manual runs.
